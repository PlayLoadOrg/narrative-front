## Acknowledgments

### Development Partnership
This project was co-developed with **Claude (Anthropic)** and **Gemini (Google)**, which served as an AI development partners throughout the design, implementation, and documentation process. The collaboration demonstrates the potential for human-AI partnership in creating educational technology.

### Subject Matter Expertise
**Special thanks to NATO subject matter experts** who provided invaluable feedback on scenario realism, operational procedures, and cognitive warfare doctrine. Their insights from real-world strategic communications operations significantly enhanced the educational value and authenticity of this simulation.

*Note: NATO review and feedback does not constitute endorsement. All opinions, scenarios, and design decisions remain the responsibility of the author.*

### Research Foundation
Narrative Front builds upon decades of research in:
- **Cognitive Psychology**: Misinformation effects and correction strategies
- **Information Science**: Narrative spread and social amplification
- **Strategic Communications**: Defensive information operations
- **Media Literacy**: Inoculation and prebunking techniques

Full citations available in `src/data/locales/en/frontopedia.json`.

### Baltic Perspective
The fictional AI generated "Filter" character draws inspiration from information environment practitioners in Estonia, Latvia, and Lithuania, whose experience on the front lines of cognitive warfare since 2007 provides crucial operational wisdom. Their unique perspective—informed by proximity to adversarial operations—shapes the tactical guidance throughout the game.

## Disclaimer

This software is provided for **educational purposes only**. It is designed to teach defensive strategic communications and media literacy skills. 

The scenarios, characters, and organizations depicted are **fictional**. Any resemblance to actual events, operations, or individuals is coincidental and used solely for pedagogical purposes.

This tool is **not a substitute** for professional training in strategic communications, information operations, or related fields. Users seeking to apply these concepts in operational contexts should pursue formal education and certification.

**Ethical Use Statement**: This project is intended to strengthen democratic information environments by improving critical thinking and resilience against manipulation. The author does not condone use of these techniques for:
- Spreading disinformation or misinformation
- Manipulating public opinion through deceptive means
- Undermining democratic institutions or processes
- Causing harm to individuals or groups

Users are responsible for ensuring their use complies with applicable laws and ethical standards in their jurisdiction.
